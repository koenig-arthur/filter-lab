What can I type?

Real decimal or scientific-notation numbers, z, parentheses, +, −, *, /, and integer powers. Both (z+1)/(z-0.8) and (1+z^-1)/(1-0.8*z^-1) work. Positive powers must combine to a causal filter. Maximum order: 64.

How are these factors interpreted?

H(z) = g z⁻ᵈ ∏(1 − zᵢ z⁻¹) / ∏(1 − pᵢ z⁻¹). A factor at the origin equals 1; it stays editable as a gray diamond but contributes no effective root. Extra origin poles or zeros come from powers of z when this expression is rewritten. Placed positions are preserved directly; roots for coefficient inputs are recovered numerically. At zero gain, placed factors and delay are retained so you can restore the gain. Arrow keys move a focused marker by 0.01; hold Shift for 0.1.
